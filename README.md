
# Prepwise — Smart Meal Prep for College Students

**Prepwise is a personalized meal-planning application designed to help college students prepare multiple meals efficiently by coordinating recipes and cooking tasks into one optimized cooking session.**

## The Problem

College students have limited time, cooking experience, and meal-planning skills. Choosing recipes and coordinating preparation steps across multiple meals can make meal prepping time-consuming and difficult to organize.

Prepwise aims to simplify this process by automatically selecting suitable recipes and generating a coordinated cooking schedule.

## Target Users

- College students with busy schedules
- Students with limited cooking experience

## Product Vision

Make meal prepping easier by reducing the time and effort required to plan, coordinate, and prepare multiple meals.

## Key Features

### MVP

- **Personalized preferences:** Account for dietary restrictions, allergies, ingredient exclusions, and cooking preferences.
- **Recipe recommendations:** Recommend recipes that match user preferences and constraints.
- **Coordinated cooking schedule:** Organize cooking tasks, dependencies, and opportunities for parallel execution.
- **Step-by-step instructions:** Provide clear tasks and estimated durations to guide users through a cooking session.

### Planned Features

- Grocery list generation
- Multi-week meal planning and calendar integration
- Recipe selection that prioritizes shared ingredients to help reduce grocery costs

## Product Success Metrics

- Number of users who successfully generate a meal-prep plan
- Percentage of users who generate another plan within 14 days
- Percentage of users who complete their recommended cooking session
- User feedback on schedule usefulness, practicality, and efficiency
- Estimated time saved compared with planning and cooking meals separately
